See how cases move through a process

Load a spreadsheet with one row per case and one timestamp column per step. A short setup asks which column is which, what the happy flow looks like, which rules apply and which variables matter. Everything else is calculated from the timestamps.

This viewer is blocking the page's scripts, so files can't be loaded here. Download the .html file and open it directly in a current browser.

Column names are free. Dates can be spreadsheet dates or text such as 2026-03-02 09:10, 02/03/2026 9:10 or 2 Mar 2026 09:10. The header row can sit below report titles. Columns that look like personal data (names, dates of birth, record numbers, contact details) are ignored unless you choose otherwise.

Each case becomes a sequence by sorting its step timestamps; an arrow means one step was directly followed by the next. Times on arrows are the gap between those two timestamps. Click a step, an arrow or a sequence to list its cases. Hover for details.

Sequences by share

Each step measured from the start step

Box: middle half of cases; teal line: median; whisker: 75th to 90th percentile. Negative times mean the step was recorded before the start step.

Last step before the end

The step completed closest before the end step is the one that last held the case up, in timestamp terms. The wait shown is from that step to the end.

Variation between groups

Each square is one group, placed by volume and by the share meeting the rule. Dashed lines show how far a group of that size can drift from the overall rate by chance. Groups below them warrant a look; groups inside them do not differ meaningfully, whatever their rank. Being outside a limit is a prompt to review, not a finding of poor practice.

Day of week by hour

Each case is counted once, in the cell for the day and hour of the chosen time. Median duration is in hours; cells with fewer than 3 cases with a value are grey. Times are read as recorded, to the minute.
